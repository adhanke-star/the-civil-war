## 2. Shiloh: five possible phases

All phase boundaries/objectives below are proposed game rules, not exact historical event times. Reuse existing packs; no new people or presence.

1. Dawn–about10:00, attack through camps. **Verified** successive Confederate lines/waves, increasingly intermixed: [Beauregard Apr11 report, deployment](https://louisiana-anthology.org/texts/beauregard/beauregard--shiloh.html), “first line”; [NPS §3](https://npshistory.com/publications/civil_war_series/22/sec3.htm), “successive waves.” Existing camps-at-dawn roster. Objective proposal: take camp line, preserve cohesion.
2. About10:00–midafternoon, center/right pressure. **Verified** Johnston died that afternoon and Beauregard succeeded; exact minutes **Disputed**. Beauregard report gives14:30; [NPS §7](https://npshistory.com/publications/civil_war_series/22/sec7.htm) agrees approximately; [NPS monument M017](https://www.nps.gov/shil/learn/historyculture/monument-detail.htm?id=M017) gives wound14:30/death14:45. Existing hornets-nest roster; current scenario retains Johnston, so historical transition behavior needs its own contract. No survival-guarantees-victory rule.
3. Midafternoon–about17:30, encircle center. **Verified** surrender, approximate scale2,200–2,300: [Prentiss OR I/10 part1 p279](https://www.civilwar.com/battles/578-official-record/series/volume/campaign/shiloh-part-I/158914-279-series-i-volume-x-i-serial-10-shiloh-part-i.html), “until 5.30 p.m.”; [NPS §8](https://npshistory.com/publications/civil_war_series/22/sec8.htm), “some 2,250 men.” Existing hornets-nest/Ruggles battery. Objective: cut retreat/accept surrender. Ruggles's gun count remains **Disputed** (traditional62 vs NPS53); do not depict the road as a trench or artillery as sole cause.
4. About17:30–dark, last ridge. **Verified** Chalmers/Jackson's failed final approach and Beauregard's halt: [Chalmers Apr12 report, closing attack](https://www.battlefields.org/learn/primary-sources/official-report-brig-gen-james-chalmers), “without success”; [NPS §9](https://npshistory.com/publications/civil_war_series/22/sec9.htm), “straight from General Beauregard.” Existing grants-last-line. Objective: limited pressure then organized disengagement; capture of Landing explicitly counterfactual.
5. Apr7 dawn–lateafternoon, counterattack/withdrawal. **Verified** Confederate withdrawal covered by Breckinridge: Beauregard report, “rear guard”; [NPS §14](https://npshistory.com/publications/civil_war_series/22/sec14.htm), “until 5:00.” Sequence supported, exact single order time **Disputed**. Existing buell-attacks ends15:00; later withdrawal/rear-guard grouping needs sourced additions and explicit mechanics.

Capture warning: Chalmers's approximately16:00 chronology conflicts with Prentiss's17:30; do not synchronize from it or add overlapping prisoner estimates. Beauregard's spoils claims are not independently established loot quantities. NPS describes smashed muskets: one prisoner is not one usable weapon.
