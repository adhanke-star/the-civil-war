# Drill reference for the soldier's hands (approved by Aaron, 2026-10-05)

Aaron saw the plates below and chose: **aiming and loading posed as drawn in the plates; shoulder arms and
marching carried on the RIGHT shoulder per the 1861 Union manual's text** (the plate book shows the older
left-shoulder carry). Every rebuilt pose must be shown to him beside the matching plate before it enters the game.

## Sources (public domain by age; originals stay at their URLs, re-download into `.out/reference/drill/`)
- **Plates:** De Witt C. Baxter, *The Volunteer's Manual* (Philadelphia: King & Baird, 1861), Scott's system,
  "one hundred illustrations". https://archive.org/details/volunteersmanual01baxt
  (page images: `https://archive.org/download/volunteersmanual01baxt/page/n<leaf>_w1100.jpg`; printed page
  numbers below, leaf numbers differ). archive.org gives no explicit rights statement; 1861 US publication.
- **Text:** W. J. Hardee, *Rifle and Light Infantry Tactics* (Nashville 1861 reprint of the 1855 edition).
  https://archive.org/details/riflelightinfant01hard. Unillustrated.
- Not found: any plate or photograph of Hardee's right-shoulder shoulder arms or right shoulder shift;
  Casey (1862) plates (a separate volume). History status: one source per item, so **Inferred**, not Verified.

## Target per game clip
| Clip | Plate (Baxter printed page, figure) | Where the hands go |
|---|---|---|
| aim, fire | p.53 Fig 93; p.52 Figs 91-92 (ready) | Hardee para 174: "the left elbow down, the right as high as the shoulder; incline the head upon the butt... the right thumb extended along the stock, the fore-finger on the trigger." Left hand forward under the stock near the lower band (seen in plate). Fire: "apply the forefinger with force to the trigger without further lowering or turning the head" (Baxter p.53). |
| ready (recover) | p.51 Figs 88-90 | Hardee paras 171-173: left hand at the lower band, thumb along the stock; "the right thumb on the head of the cock, the other fingers under and against the guard", then "seize the piece at the small of the stock". |
| load | p.45 Figs 74-76; p.46 Figs 77-79; p.48 Fig 84 | Butt on the ground, muzzle opposite the centre of the body, piece touching the left thigh, left hand at the middle band; right hand to the cartridge box (Baxter p.45). Cartridge seized with thumb and next two fingers. Rammer drawn "between the thumb and forefinger bent, the other fingers shut" (p.46); ramming with "the thumb and forefinger bent, the other fingers closed" (p.48; Hardee para 163, back of the hand to the front, elbows near the body). |
| stand (shoulder arms) | no correct plate (p.24 Figs 12-15 show the older LEFT carry: do not copy) | Hardee para 121 (p.29): "The piece in the right hand, the barrel nearly vertical, and resting in the hollow of the shoulder, the guard to the front, the arm hanging nearly at its full length near the body; the thumb and fore-finger embracing the guard, the remaining fingers closed together, and grasping the swell of the stock just under the cock, which rests on the little finger." Left hand hangs at the side. |
| walk (march) | none | Same as shoulder arms (the game's march clip). Hardee's right shoulder shift (paras 210-211, text only) is an alternative if Aaron later wants it. |
| order arms (not a game clip yet) | p.32 Fig 35 | Hardee paras 147-149: "The hand low, the barrel between the thumb and fore-finger, extended along the stock; the other fingers extended and joined"; butt against the toe of the right foot. |

Hand shape rule (Aaron: fingers were "bent oddly", hands "in the wrong spot"; prefer simple): one natural
closed hand for every grip, placed exactly where the table says; only the trigger forefinger, the thumb along
the stock in aim, and the thumb-and-forefinger rammer hold differ from it.
