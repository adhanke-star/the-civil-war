      Controls

      • Select: click or tap a brigade or its flag (Tab cycles flags; Shift+Enter adds or removes a friendly flag); Shift+drag on the ground, or hold a finger still then drag, to box-select several
      • March: drag from your brigade and release; it halts to fight any enemy that comes in range on the way, then marches on. Hold Shift (or twist a second finger) to set the facing. Right-click ground for a straight march
      • Attack: release the drag on an enemy: the brigade closes to effective range, halts and fires, and follows if the enemy moves. Only Charge goes in with the bayonet
      • Keyboard march: select with a flag or Army list, press B, then arrows to move the destination 25 m (Shift: 5 m). Q/E turn its facing 15° (Shift: 5°). Enter orders; Escape cancels the ghost and keeps your selection
      • Keyboard attack: T chooses or cycles enemy targets; Shift+T cycles backwards. Enter orders ranged fire facing that target. B switches to march; ? opens this help. Starting or cancelling a ghost issues no order
      • Camera: drag the ground or two-finger scroll to pan; pinch or wheel to zoom toward the pointer (the view tilts as you zoom); right-drag or Option + scroll to turn; WASD / arrows, Q/E, +/-; double-click or double-tap a flag to fly to it; click the map
      • Reading the field: under each flag is what the brigade is doing; the bar under the flag is its strength, striped when shaken, broken when wavering, hollow when routing
      • Orders: H hold · C charge · R run · F fall back · X halt · V hold fire · Space pause · 1/2/3 speed · M map · L army list
